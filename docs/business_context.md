# Business Context

The project analyzes an FMCG product offer from a wholesale or retail environment. The dataset includes product names, stock quantities, units of measure, VAT rates, standard net and gross prices, alternative prices and creation timestamps.

In a real business environment, this type of data supports sales, purchasing, pricing, controlling and operational reporting.

## Business Objectives

- Understand the size and structure of the product offer.
- Monitor product availability.
- Identify missing, zero or invalid prices.
- Check whether gross prices match net prices and VAT rates.
- Detect products with alternative pricing.
- Prepare a clean reporting layer for Power BI.

## Target Users

- Sales managers
- Purchasing teams
- Pricing analysts
- Inventory analysts
- BI analysts
- Operations managers

## Analytical Value

The dataset is useful because it connects product master data, price lists and availability in one table. This allows the analysis to cover both data quality and business performance.
